What it does

A small, quiet guardian
for the eyes you stare with all day.

EyeBreak lives in your menu bar and counts down quietly. Every twenty minutes it asks you to look up — at something twenty feet away, for twenty seconds. The full screen dims to a warm, low-glare overlay; a soft tone plays; a countdown ticks. Then it gets out of your way.

Every ten minutes, a smaller nudge reminds you to sit up straight. EyeBreak knows when you're on a call or presenting — mic, camera, screen-share, all detected — and stays silent during meetings. You can skip a break, add five minutes if you're mid-thought, or change the cadence to whatever your body actually wants.

It's the kind of utility you stop noticing after a week. Then your eyes stop hurting at 4pm and you wonder why.

Features

Built for real work days.

The 20-20-20 rule

Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds. The eye-care standard, automated.

Posture nudges

Every 10 minutes, a small reminder to sit up straight. Off by default during calls.

Smart call detection

Mic, camera, and screen-share are all sensed. EyeBreak stays silent when you're presenting.

Skip · or add 5 minutes

One click to defer. EyeBreak reminds, it doesn't lock you out of your own work.

Activity rings & streaks

Apple-Watch-style rings for week consistency, daily breaks, and minutes rested. With streaks.

Apple Health integration

Mindful minutes from each completed break sync to Apple Health, so the rest counts where it matters.

Custom intervals

20 minutes is the default; pick anything. Same for the hourly posture cadence.

Soothing tones

A synthesized flute melody, or a small bell. Pick the one that fits your room.

Why I built it

Because my eyes kept telling me.

I stare at screens longer than I admit to. The 20-20-20 rule has been around forever, but every app I tried either nagged me through meetings, looked like a 2014 Windows utility, or locked me out of my own laptop. So I built the one I wanted: warm, quiet, Mac-native, smart enough to know when to shut up.

EyeBreak is the small, calm thing in the corner that's been quietly looking out for me all day. I hope it does the same for you.

— Vikas
