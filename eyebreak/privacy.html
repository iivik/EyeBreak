EyeBreak

Privacy Policy.

Last updated · May 2026

Overview

EyeBreak is a local-first macOS app. It runs on your Mac, in your menu bar, and quietly counts down to your next eye break. We built EyeBreak with a simple privacy stance: your screen-time habits are nobody's business but yours. EyeBreak does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. EyeBreak does not require an account, login, or email address. No personally identifiable information is collected by the app.

What stays on your Mac

Everything. EyeBreak stores the following only in macOS UserDefaults on your machine, and never sends them off your device:

  • Your settings (break interval, posture interval, sound choice, theme).
  • A 7-day rolling history of break counts and rested seconds, used to render activity rings and streaks. Older entries are pruned automatically.
  • Trial start date, used to compute trial state locally.
  • Purchase status, returned by Apple's StoreKit and stored locally.

System permissions EyeBreak uses

To detect when you're on a call (so EyeBreak doesn't interrupt you), the app reads system signals — microphone activity, camera activity, and screen-sharing process state — using standard macOS APIs (CoreAudio, CoreMediaIO, NSWorkspace). These signals are read on-device for the moment EyeBreak needs them, and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted. EyeBreak never accesses microphone audio or camera frames.

Apple Health integration

With your explicit permission, EyeBreak writes Mindful Minutes to Apple Health for each completed break. EyeBreak only writes — it never reads your Health data. You can revoke this permission at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

In-app purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Apple via StoreKit. EyeBreak never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction itself: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Analytics, tracking, advertising

EyeBreak contains no analytics, no tracking pixels, no telemetry, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers. The app does not phone home.

Children's privacy

EyeBreak is a productivity utility safe for general audiences. Because EyeBreak collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Data deletion

To remove every trace of EyeBreak from your Mac, drag the app to Trash and empty it. To clear stored stats and settings without uninstalling, choose Reset all data in EyeBreak's About panel.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's About panel on next launch.
